# 32 — Every frame of the owner's footage (Build 33)

The owner's second video package: 17 original videos (Feb 2017 – Nov 2019), 13,692 frames at
2 fps (frame N at (N−1)/2 s, 640×360), read in full by eight reviewers, one or two videos each.
The frames and the per-video reviews are private (`local/wiki-raw/research-b33/camera-pack/`);
this page keeps facts and numbers, each with its video and frames. About 250 frame files in the
package are empty; their neighbours cover them. F = seen in the frames.

## 1. Tactical battle camera (F)

- **Default view:** side-on, slightly 3/4, chest height, allies on the left third. It does not
  move during input (ZCUC 289–455, x0kb a 25 s idle). The "settle" after an action or at an area
  start is a full-screen motion blur while the HUD appears; the framing before START! and at the
  first input is pixel-identical (ZCUC 285→290 and four more).
- **Normal attacks and blue skills** stay close to the default view: pushed in 15–25 % with a pan
  (x0kb throughout; WAwk), or a side-on two-shot, sometimes one cut low behind the attacker (ZCUC
  460–463 Kars, 898–904). Many play with no cut at all (x0kb).
- **Enemy normal attacks:** some cut low from the party's side with the near ally drawn as a
  see-through silhouette (WAwk 284, 316, 528); others, the boss's walk-up hits among them, play in
  the default view.
- **Red (Stand) SP skills and time stops** cut between angles on the battlefield, HUD visible:
  behind-right over the shoulder, a low front three-quarter, the reverse from the enemy side, a
  90° side shot, and a high wide view 45–60° down (ZCUC 930–952, 1009–1019, 1072–1080; x0kb
  166–233, 515–536; X9xS Jotaro SP; the road roller nearly straight down).
- **In-battle cinematics are short cut-ins** of 1.5–2.5 s (x0kb 222–224, 266–268, 390–394; X9xS
  Hol Horse), or a full SP cinematic of about 11 s on its own stage (ZCUC 930–952 Kars). The long
  compilation clips (lLle, NXtS) are those full cinematics.
- **Support buffs:** a two-shot of caster and buffed ally, then back to the default view before
  the caption (ZCUC, h6Hs 1:23–1:30). **Counters:** a wide low shot along the ground from the
  party's side (p1zD).
- **KO:** when an enemy is retired the view turns 60–90° to look at it for 1–1.5 s (x0kb 30, 79,
  126, 284), and its portrait gets a 「再起不能 リタイア」 stamp.
- **Reversal activation:** no cut. **Reserve swap-in:** no cut; the new member lands crouched in
  the default view (X9xS 557, 682).
- **Boss intro:** a low shot tracking the boss's legs (all videos); in 2018 then a hard cut to the
  default view (ZCUC 1060–1062, p1zD); in 2019 a crane up, a profile and a tilted low knee-up
  shot with the card 「AREA BOSS」 and the name (WAwk, h6Hs; x0kb 431; X9xS).
- **Stage finish:** the camera on the ground beside the last enemy killed, not always the boss
  (ZCUC 1083, WAwk, h6Hs); a time-stop finish shows 「OverKill」 in negative colours (X9xS).
- **Win pose:** the character who landed the final blow, in every tactical case (ZCUC both
  battles, p1zD 294–301, WAwk, h6Hs). A tight head-and-chest shot from well below, the winner
  right of centre turned towards WIN!, settling over about 0.5–1 s (ZCUC 636–646, 1093–1102; X9xS
  753–761).

## 2. Tactical mechanics seen (F)

- Chain bonus ×1.1 / ×1.35 / ×1.5 / ×2 from 4 chains (ZCUC, WAwk, h6Hs, X9xS); x0kb shows ×2 at 6.
  The chain resets when the party is hit (ZCUC).
- TURN counts every unit action, ally or enemy, and carries over between areas (X9xS 1→57).
- Reversal: the R fills with orange from the bottom and pulses white-gold when full (x0kb 248);
  activation captions 「4ターンの間」 「クールターン半減！」 「スピード200%アップ！」; cool turns halve at
  once; 「あと n ターン」 counts the active turns down (4 → 1); enemies still act.
- Time stop: a short one around one attack without a banner (DIO's WRYYY skill, X9xS), and the
  The World turn stop with the banner 「世界(ザ・ワールド)！ 時止めの発動」 that fills the turn bar
  with the user's cards (ZCUC 604–619; X9xS TURN 3→7; enemy Jotaro the same, ZCUC).
- Diavolo's SP turns the stage into a cosmos for about 3 min (h6Hs 312–671); Reversal shatters it
  (413). King Crimson's cut-in erases the enemy attack it fires on.
- White Album's cinematic is a counter, fired by enemy ranged attacks while
  「遠距離攻撃へのカウンター待ち受け状態×3」 is active (p1zD, 4 times, 4–4.5 s each).
- Reticle labels 「有利」/「不利」; red enemy countdowns on the turn bar; cool turns as dark squares
  with a number; blue = スキル, pink = SPスキル (ZCUC tutorial).
- Event points: 850000 first clear + 1650000 special = 2500000 (x0kb).

## 3. Action battle (F)

- Follow camera behind and above (about 40–50° down), never cutting in combat (mRvY); some areas
  are side-on like a side-scroller (pKz0 589–647); bends turn the camera smoothly by 30–90°; in
  2019 melee it sits closer and lower (q-eq).
- Only a quest's first area has intro shots of the other members; later areas start in gameplay
  without START! (mRvY, pKz0). Boss card 「AREA BOSS」; a black brush wipe after it (mRvY).
- ドドドモード: a 2D face cut-in on a slanted band; the stage colour change differs by video
  (none in mRvY, indigo for ~1.5 s in XdsYY, recoloured in pKz0/q-eq).
- Character switch: a pink light pillar and the portraits reorder (q-eq, mRvY). Support call:
  the support appears and attacks, then about 7 s of cinematic (mRvY, support DIO).
- Win: in the last area's location with normal colours; the active character at the finish poses
  (mRvY Zeppeli, q-eq Esidisi).

## 4. Menus (F)

- No menu screen moves its camera (Y5E9, 6RmF, XdsYY).
- Team screen: on entry the models load LEADER → MAIN → RESERVE (Y5E9 779–786); team HP/ATK/DEF
  and Mind capacity are the sums of the three members.
- Statue detail: the model full-body on a pedestal; the Stand only for Stand users; the floating
  frames around it hold the equipped Mind cards (Y5E9 1:26–1:48).
- AP maximum = rank + 19 (6RmF rank 100 → 119, Y5E9 rank 109 → 128, pKz0 rank 37 → 56).
- My Page: the bottom-left plate is a fixed line, not a ticker; the first icon is メッセージボックス;
  banners change every 5 s. Also seen: quick formation (copy, paste, disband), assist formation
  with per-statue values, Arena teams with 「BPコスト」, statue enhancement (Lv 1→18 previewed, 22
  on 「大成功ッ!!」).
- Title after the shutdown: client 3.12.0; the end-of-service notice has no buttons; START blinks
  about every 1.5 s (1umh).

## 5. Gacha (F)

- The July 2018 「超降臨ガシャ」: ★6 total 1.500 % (DIO 0.100 %, six others 0.233 % each), ★5 total
  3.000 % over 50 statues; 5 diamonds a single draw, 50 for ten, 30 for a Mind ten-draw; the
  guaranteed ★4+ is the first of the ten (aiwo 144–165, 1127–1152). Ours keeps the standard
  banner's published rates (★5 1.5 %, ★4 12.5 %, ★3 86 %) and free draws.
- The opening store shot and the object Crazy Diamond holds between reveals hint the rarity in
  2018 (aiwo, 9ENm); in 2017 the same shots appeared on ★3 draws (mRvY), so they are not a
  reliable sign.
- Duplicates become awakening items (覚醒の偶像, silver/gold/rainbow by rarity); only ten-draws
  have a skip button (「結果にブッ飛ばす」).
